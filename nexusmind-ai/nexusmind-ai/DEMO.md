# Live demo script (about 4 minutes)
1. Overview: counts come from the stored data. Say the 3 papers are real arXiv papers with paraphrased claims and short abstract quotes; the cross-paper links are AI-inferred.
2. Knowledge Graph: click a finding node to show its source paper, page and excerpt.
3. Research Detective: ask "How do language models use long context, and what extends it?" and show the cited evidence and the conflict flag. Then ask something unrelated to show the "insufficient evidence" answer.
4. Contradiction Radar: mark the inferred tension "not a contradiction" or "unresolved" and explain it is a candidate, not a finding.
5. Research Gap Explorer: show the repeated limitation and the "candidate gap, needs human review" caveat.
6. Hypothesis Studio: draft a hypothesis; point out the source-supported vs AI-proposed labels.
7. Reports: generate, then download Markdown and PDF.
Before judging: start the backend, check Settings says "Backend connected", and have `/docs` open as a backup.
